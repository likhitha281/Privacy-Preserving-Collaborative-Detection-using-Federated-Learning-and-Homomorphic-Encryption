## Label flipping by one bank (`label_flip`)

Files: [label_flip.png](label_flip.png), [label_flip.pdf](label_flip.pdf)

Sources: `attacks/label_flip.csv`

Takeaway: At f=0.25 (3 banks, FedAvg): F1@0.5 drops 0.212, tuned F1 drops 0.004, PR-AUC drops 0.031. Most of the F1@0.5 loss is a threshold shift (scores pushed above 0.5), which the validation-tuned threshold undoes. At f=1 PR-AUC drops by 0.153-0.388. FedAvg vs FedAvg+CKKS drops differ by at most 0.005 (encryption neither helps nor hides the damage from the evaluator).
